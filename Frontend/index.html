What is HackGrid?

An evidence-grounded AI platform for technical & regulatory intelligence.

HackGrid helps analysts, engineers, and policymakers solve complex technical, regulatory, policy, security, and operational questions. Unlike conventional AI systems that treat an LLM's generated response as the source of truth, HackGrid operates on a strict principle: evidence first, AI-assisted reasoning second, and human authority wherever decisions matter.

"HackGrid helps people make difficult technology and security decisions by showing what the available evidence actually supports."

Core Intelligence Workflow
    Evidence Philosophy

    Distinguishing What Is Established From What Is Inferred

    To prevent hallucinated conclusions and premature attribution, HackGrid strictly delineates between direct statutory proof, analytical deductions, and unproven hypotheses.

    Established Direct Proof
    What the retrieved evidence directly supports

    Explicit statutory clauses, verified standard parameters, valid container signatures, or benchmark requirements directly cited from authoritative corpora with chunk provenance.

    Inferred Analytical Deduction
    What can reasonably be concluded

    Logical conclusions drawn where technical requirements and national rules intersect, evaluated across supporting and contextual evidence, labeled with explicit confidence bounds.

    Unresolved Missing / Qualified
    What available evidence does not establish

    Operational gaps where mandatory incident triggers are unproven, telemetry is incomplete, or competing root-cause hypotheses remain open pending operational forensics.

    The Intelligence Pipeline

    An intelligence pipeline, not a chat window.

    HackGrid rejects the idea of treating an LLM as an unconstrained authority. Instead, an 8-stage verifiable pipeline examines evidence provenance, tests counter-hypotheses, evaluates statutory consequence, and enforces human governance.

    Architectural Invariant

    "AI synthesizes evidence. Evidence supports the conclusion. Human authority governs consequential decisions."

      Counter-Evidence Framework

      Active Counter-Evidence Reasoning

      Instead of merely asking "What evidence supports this conclusion?", HackGrid systematically retrieves and evaluates what weakens, contradicts, or limits it.

      Supporting Evidence

      Directly corroborates the technical finding, parameter baseline, or statutory duty with cited passages.

      Contradicting Evidence

      Directly conflicts with a claim, invalidates an assumption, or disproves premature attribution.

      Limiting Evidence

      Restricts scope (e.g., valid vendor signatures and clean SBOMs limiting supply-chain compromise claims).

      Contextual Evidence

      Provides operational environment background without establishing direct causal or legal liability.

      Collaborative Multi-Agent Intelligence

      Specialized analytical roles examining the same evidence.

      HackGrid's multi-agent architecture is not a collection of independent chatbots. Instead, specialized analytical components interrogate different dimensions of the same technical and regulatory question over a shared, verified evidence set.

      Architectural Principle

      "These components do not independently become the final authority. Their findings converge into an integrated, evidence-grounded assessment."

              Experimental AI Research

              A Research Platform for Evidence-Grounded AI & Governance.

              HackGrid is an experimental research platform investigating how artificial intelligence can reason over high-stakes, safety-critical, and legally binding systems. Rather than building another generative chatbot, our research focuses on verifiable retrieval, consequence-driven governance, and traceable human-AI decision systems.

              System Theory

              The 7-Layer Conceptual Stack

              Every query moves through seven explicit architectural boundaries. Each layer answers a distinct question before passing structured state to the next:

              01 · KnowledgeCorpus Layer
              "What does the system know?"

              Multi-domain indexed knowledge bases (Telecom, Maritime, Defence) with chunk-level metadata and verified document provenance.

              02 · RetrievalVector Layer
              "Which evidence is relevant?"

              BGE-M3 1024-dim dense embeddings and FAISS index search with quota-based multi-KB candidate pooling.

              03 · EvidenceValidation Layer
              "Is the evidence sufficient, applicable, and trustworthy?"

              Provenance checking, absence safeguards (preventing false negative inferences), and dual counter-evidence retrieval.

              04 · ReasoningMulti-Agent Layer
              "What does the evidence actually imply?"

              Specialized analytical roles (Network, Compliance, Policy, Gap) decomposing evidence without unilateral authority.

              05 · RiskEvaluation Layer
              "How consequential is the decision?"

              Deterministic Risk Engine scoring decision severity, evidence certainty, operational impact, and required authority tiers.

              06 · GovernancePolicy Invariants
              "Who should be allowed to make the decision?"

              Inviolable governance rules enforcing mandatory human sign-off on critical risks, regulatory notices, and operational commands.

              07 · Human DecisionAuthority Layer
              "When should AI stop and a human take over?"

              A qualified reviewer inspects verified evidence, confirms, modifies, or rejects the assessment, recorded to the audit trail.
